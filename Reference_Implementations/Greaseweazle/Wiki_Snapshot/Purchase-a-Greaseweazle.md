## Greaseweazle Merch

T shirts and other merchandise are available via Spreadshirt, shipping from
the EU and USA. These shops make no profit for the project; they exist
just for fun and to spread the Greaseweazle word!

 - EU shop: [greaseweazle.myspreadshop.co.uk](https://greaseweazle.myspreadshop.co.uk/all)
 - USA shop: [greaseweazle.myspreadshop.com](https://greaseweazle.myspreadshop.com/all)

Further notes:
1. The EU shop is a UK domain with UK pricing. However the shirts are
manufactured in Germany. Hence there will be no import taxes to pay
when shipping within the EU.
2. Conversely, UK shipping may attract
import tax if the value is over £135. However Spreadshirt promise to
refund these taxes if you contact them.
3. The T Shirt design is
tested. I have not seen a hoodie or mug in the flesh. Send me pictures
if you order one!
4. Spreadshirt have various other garments and items available for
splashing with the Greaseweazle logo. Let me know if there are any
specific items you would like to see added from their range.
5. Finally: Always click the discount-code banner. (There always seems to
be one!)
