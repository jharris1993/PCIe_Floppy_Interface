**Greaseweazle** is a USB device and host tools allowing versatile
floppy drive control. By extracting the raw flux transitions from a
drive, any disk format can be captured and analysed: PC, Amiga,
Amstrad, PDP-11, musical instruments, industrial equipment, and
more. Greaseweazle also supports writing to floppy disks, from a range
of image file formats including those commonly used for online
preservation (ADF, IPF, DSK, IMG, HFE, ...).
